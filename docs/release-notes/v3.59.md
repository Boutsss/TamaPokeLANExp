A small quality-of-life update. Your save carries over. Install as usual: the installer backs your save up first and never erases it.

## Catching

- **See what you just caught.** After a successful catch, tapping past the "caught!" message now opens the new Pokemon's STATS page straight away, instead of dropping you back on Explore. One tap takes you to its sheet (moves, typing, and the buttons to raise it or let it go); backing out from there leaves you on the Party screen. If your party was full, it opens the PC box entry the catch went into.

## PC box

- **Look at any boxed Pokemon, even with a full party.** Tapping a Pokemon in the box always opens its sheet now, so you can check its stats and moves without moving it anywhere. Before, a full party skipped the sheet and went straight to asking who to swap out. To bring it into a full party, tap TO PARTY on its sheet and then pick who steps out.

Nothing to do on your side: install and play.
