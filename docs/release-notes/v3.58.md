A small battle and bag update. Your save carries over. Install as usual: the installer backs your save up first and never erases it.

## Bag

- **Every item now says what it does.** In the bag, each item shows a short line under its name -- "FIRE moves x1.2", "Heals 1/16 of its HP every turn", "ELECTRIC moves x1.2; ARCEUS turns ELECTRIC" -- so you know what you are giving a creature before it holds it.

## Battles

- **Choice items show their lock.** Once a creature holding a Choice Band, Specs or Scarf has used a move, that move is marked LOCKED and the others are greyed out. Tapping a greyed move now says why instead of quietly using the locked one.
- **Taunt and the Assault Vest grey out status moves** the same way, so the menu never offers a move the turn would refuse.
- **Arceus shows its plate type.** When Arceus holds a plate, its battle panel shows the type it has become, so you can see its new weaknesses at a glance.

Nothing to do on your side: install and play.
