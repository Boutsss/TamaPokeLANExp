A big one: everything from v3.29 to v3.57 arrives at once. Your save carries over. Install as usual: the installer backs your save up first and never erases it.

## Battles

- **Every battle held item from Gens 1-7** -- Choice Band, Life Orb, Leftovers, Assault Vest, the type boosters, gems, plates, resist berries and about 160 more, each working as it does in the games. (No Z-crystals, mega stones or form-changing items.)
- **Arceus and the plates**: a plate turns Arceus into that type for the fight -- its weaknesses and its same-type bonus included.
- Hundreds more moves (every Gen 1-7 move the battle engine can play faithfully), including switching moves like U-TURN, ROAR and BATON PASS, and real status moves.
- Held items are never used up for good: a berry or a sash works once per battle and is back afterwards.

## Battle Tower

- **The Battle Tower has its own page** on the swipe, after the gyms.
- **Rewards by tier**: floors 1-10 pay common items, better ones from floor 11, and the best (Choice items, Life Orb, Leftovers...) start appearing from floor 50. Floor 100 pays four top-tier items. Past 100 it goes on forever: a Master Ball every floor and a top-tier item every 5th.
- **Your climb is saved.** The team you start a run with is kept for the whole climb, so you can change your party in between. LATER walks away and comes back to the same floor; running from a fight, or the board switching off mid-fight, brings you back to that floor to try again. Only losing (or END RUN, which asks twice) ends a run.

## The world

- **The Arcade has its own page**, between the Mart and the gyms, with six games (now including PINBALL and SURF) -- and you can play while your pet sleeps.
- In PINBALL the BACK button moved to the top corner, off the flippers.
- **A new Pokedex look**: a red Pokedex with its lens and lights, and an LCD screen for the region list, the grid and every entry.
- **The gyms page is an arena**: each region shows its eight badges, earned ones in colour.
- **Eggs hatch by walking**: 200 steps.
- **Daycare**, **trading** and **friends** (gifts and visits) between two boards.
- **A PC box of 1,200 slots** in 40 named boxes, with filters, sorting, favourites, MOVE and SEARCH. Starring a favourite is a tap; taking the star off is a 2-second hold, so it never happens by accident.
- **Natures** for every creature, with a boost and a drop.
- Daily tasks now refresh **every 12 hours**.
- The Mart sells Poke Balls ($50), Great Balls ($100) and Ultra Balls ($200); healing items come from wild battles instead, and berries can drop there too. No healing items in the Battle Tower or LAN battles.
- A **trainer card** with stars for big achievements, a badges button, ranks, and five boy and five girl starting looks to choose from.
- A **rainbow star** for catching 10 shiny Pokemon (counted from this version on).
- Swapped trainer cards show the other player's **stars**, and any they earned since their last card **flash** until you tap.
- A shiny you **catch or get in a trade** now shows its shiny sprite in the Pokedex.
- A **how-to-play card** for new players, and a **held BACK button** on every screen below the pet, so a stray tap can no longer drop you out of a game.

## Fixes

- The STATS page shows each creature's current numbers.
- Many smaller layout and touch fixes found on real boards.

Nothing to do but install. If anything looks wrong afterwards, your save backup is in the file the installer downloaded.
